WHO WE ARE

Faith, Knowledge, and Service

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a Catholic educational institution committed to providing meaningful learning experiences guided by Christian values.

KCC aims to help students develop their knowledge, abilities, character, and sense of responsibility. Through education rooted in faith and service, the institution encourages learners to become responsible members of society.

  • Promoting quality Catholic education.
  • Developing Christian values and good character.
  • Encouraging service and responsibility toward others.

OUR HISTORY

A Tradition of Catholic Education

Kabankalan Catholic College has a long-standing role in education and community formation in Kabankalan City. Its story reflects a continuing commitment to learning, faith, and service.

Through the years, the KCC community has continued to value education as an important part of personal growth and social responsibility.
